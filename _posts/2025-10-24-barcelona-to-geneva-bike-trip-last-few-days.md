Barcelona to Geneva  bike trip last few days
Stopped biking a few days ago due to stormy weather and am currently in Berlin seeing some museums (Stasi), a comedy club, street art, and wall remnants.  Flying home Monday from Frankfurt.
Last few days:
Rode through the mountains to Valence (France) and stayed in a cool bike hostel with chickens in the back yard. A stormy week was coming up so decided against continuing in the mountains.
Rode the Via Rhona to Lyon then Geneva.  This is a 400+ mile long bike path along the Rhône River from Marseille on the Mediterranean to Geneva in Switzerland.  Worthwhile and a great way to start bike touring in Europe.   Was striking coming into the Rhone river valley because it was essentially the first cold clean surface water I had seen on the trip.  Passed by a women’s “tough mudder” style competition, many swans, a high school whitewater kayak race in a channel formed by a hydro dam, and two nuclear power plants.
Stayed in Lyon two nights. Fun city and stayed in the cool Flanders Hostel. The local music conservancy had a birthday party for one of the students with an impromptu jazz concert in the hostel bar.  There was also a big  film festival with Sean Penn and Natalie Portman in town.
Visited the Saxons  from our ski lodge  (Meany) in Lausanne (Switzerland) and bikepacking friends  in Basel who I had met in Baja 3 years ago.
Overall, rode about 800 miles, which sounds like a lot but it was over many days.  The mountains and road conditions slow things down considerably but add a lot of variety.  The bike trails speed things up a lot and have nice amenities (charging stations, bathrooms, picnic tables) and of course it’s fun to be on a bike highway.  Tradeoffs.
