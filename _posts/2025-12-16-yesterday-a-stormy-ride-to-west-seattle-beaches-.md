Yesterday, a stormy ride to West Seattle beaches, parks, ravines, and water taxi.  69 parks left out of 430
Looped from my house down through downtown to West Seattle to visit the places on the west side of West Seattle.
The beach parks are great, and it was a very windy day with big waves and then the sun peaking out.
The highlight was the King County Water Taxi back to downtown.  Oddly I was the only passenger, I guess since commuters are going the other way.  The water taxi is a cool, comfortable fast boat and only takes an orca card to get on.  2 blocks from the Pioneer Square link stop.
Visited:
Ercolini Park - playground
Morgan Junction
Pelly Place Natural Area - a steep ravine with trail up into it.
Lowman Beach park - great beachfront.  Also called capsize park, presumably because of the rough water in wind.
Emma Schmitz Memorial Overlook
Me-Kwa-Mooks Park
Adover Place - a little path to a small beach.
Cormorant cove
Charles Richey Sr Viewpoint
West Seattle Lighthouse - fenced off and hard to see
Seacrest Park/Water Taxi terminal - also has a fishing dock, good for squidding in winter
