Rainy Christmas night loop in North East Seattle, to spots in Wedgwood, Lake City, Pinehurst, Victory Heights.
I’m both delighted and embarassed to visit several spots tonight that I’ve never been to, even though I’ve basically lived around this part of town for my whole life.
Lake City is underrated in my view, even though it’s a bit less useful now that the Fred Meyer and Bartells is gone.
Lake city Park/Pea Patch - newish spot amongst tall buildings. A small basketball court, p-patch, playground, grassy area. Nice.
Lake City Mini Park - fenced off and closed for now.  I think maybe it should be open and have some police on foot walking around the neighborhood a bit (would also be useful in the U District and 12th/Jackson).
Lake City Memorial Triangle - nice tree on the main corner.
Albert Davis Park between the library and community center and tent city 4.  Albert Davis was a community organizer who started the annual Lake City salmon bbq
Pinehurst Playground - has neat shelter space for rent
Pinehurst Pocket park - corner of a block.
Victory Creek park - a trib of Thornton Creek, exposed on the east side of the Northgate QFC.
Victory Heights Playground  - never knew about it. Nice
Mock Creek Ravine on 35th and 97th.  Never noticed it despite driving by thousands of times. It reveals a tributary of Thornton Creek and is mostly surrounded by private property.
