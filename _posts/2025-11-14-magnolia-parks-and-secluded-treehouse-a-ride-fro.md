Magnolia parks and secluded treehouse.  A ride from a few days ago.
Ran into a Google waymo car making images for google maps. It is taking images of every street in the world and I am taking pictures of every park in Seattle.
Obviously when you think of Magnolia, Discovery park and Magnolia Boulevard come to mind, but the little spots on the south shore of Magnolia are really cool.
In an arc:
Thorndyke Park - a cool biggish widespot with a view
Ursula Judkins Viewpoint!
Smith Cove and Open Water park - great public beach and waterfront  access. Quiet.
Magnolia Tidelands and the trail over to 32nd Ave Beach Access.  At low tide you can walk west to the treehouse (currently for sale) that was on the treehouse masters show.  Google levitating lighthouse.
The neighborhood above Magnolia Blvd with its madrona trees and various spots: Eastmont, Carleton Center, Parkmont place.  This is a fun place to explore.
Discovery Park lighthouse!!
You could have a great in-city hike walking along the south side of Magnolia.
