I’m in Florida riding 300 miles from Tallahassee to Gainesville on the Forgotten coast route. This is an extension of the Florida Divide ride I did last year.
Why Florida?  It’s accessible and fun to explore in the winter. And completely different than my home state of Washington.  Florida is flat, shallow coasts, swampy, warm. Washington, rocky, mountainous, deep coasts, cold in winter.
Except that it’s freezing temps in Tally these days. Tally is a cool college town, with Florida State, Florida A and M and also the Capitol of Florida.  Neat place built on one of the few hills around here.
Day 1 followed a series of mountain bike trails out of Tally, eventually making it to the National forest and got to “Pope Still” camp late.  It’s hunting season (you’d think I would have checked on the timing of that) and lots of people with dogs are out hunting deer and camping.
Now I’m in Sopchoppy and heading west towards St Marks wildlife refuge.
