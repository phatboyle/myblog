Hello all - I did most of the Spain section of EDT 18 months ago. Now I'm planning riding north from the Barcelona area north into France and Germany, starting in mid April.
I've noticed that there are three versions of the route.  There is the version on ridewithgps that is linked off
bikepacking.com
website
https://ridewithgps.com/routes/36198954
Also there are two on Komoot:
one in Andy's profile:
https://www.komoot.com/collection/1012541/-european-divide-trail-fastest-known-time
And a different one linked off the
europeandivide.com
website:
https://www.komoot.com/collection/1070173/7-600-km-across-the-continent-european-divide-trail?utm_campaign=collection_embed&utm_medium=embed&utm_source=europeandividetrail.com
They follow the same trajectory but if you overlay them on a map there are areas, like in France where I am looking, that have  differences between each (forest road vs trail vs regular road to get through a specific area).  Based on my studies, I'm not going to use the rwgps version and I'll use both Komoot ones as I think (though not sure) they are more recent and definately better documented.
I think by comparing the two at each stage I'll be able to make judgements and pick options.
I'm interested in other's thoughts.
