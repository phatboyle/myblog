ABK is in Hood River the week of August 11 and August 18.   Andy Brandt and his crew teaches pretty much all skills of windsurfign and winging and this is a highly worthwhile class for people at any level.  $700 for 5 days.Here is a longer description borrowed from some posts I made in the past:Full disclosure - My family has been doing this camp for 8 years, and my kids (college age) sometimes help out at the camps.   I feel like my family lucked into finding Andy just in time when my kids were in middle school and becoming skeptical about me teaching them.  8 years later, we look forward every summer to seeing many of the same faces, and some new ones every year for a week in Hood River.
https://www.abkboardsports.com/camps
Andy’s camps are unique and a great place for sailors of all abilities to get better.  Whether you are learning to waterstart or doing high wind freestyle moves, he and his crew can help.
The  class is 5 days at the Marina in Hood River, from Monday to Friday, 9:30-5 with a 2 hour lunch.  About ⅓ of the time is spent in lectures ranging from normal progression skills such as waterstarting or jibing, to foiling and winging.  The other ⅔ is on the water.  It costs $700 and you need to bring your own gear.  Usually about 12-20 people are in the class (note that he has a reasonable cancellation policy if your plans change).

So if you like windsurfing or winging, or windfoiling, I’d encourage you to show up with your high wind gear, and a borrowed bigger board for lightwind freestyle practice. It’s  a great camp to take with your kid, spouse, parent or friend because they will work with everyone at their ability level.If the wind is light, you might spend the morning working on sailing clew first, backwinding, duck jibes, or depowering the sail for heli tacks and upwind 360s.  When the wind picks up you might find that clew first practice helps you exit your jibes a lot more reliably.  His goal is to teach a bunch of things to practice and have fun with, no matter what the conditions are.
Ask around and I think you’ll hear similar stories from others who have taken his class over the years - his clinic is worthwhile, and you'll get better because he makes you think and gives you things to work on.
