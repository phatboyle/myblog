Spain France ride, day 8, ~ 300 miles
Met a dog today: I was riding along a fenced orchard today when I saw a large dog running loose.  I stopped and it seemed friendly though I was puzzled why it was out. I kept going and the dog followed me for a mile into the hills outside of town.  I got worried then noticed a whats app number on its collar.  A guy answered and quickly drove out to my location. He said the dog gets out through holes under the fence dug by boars, and likes to follow bikers.   I kind of miss it, but sure am glad it got home so easily.
Got to France today on the day where the news says there is no government though as far as I can tell, everything is working fine.  The border was  at a nice pass with some crumbling and non crumbling guard towers. I was expecting at least a border sign but alas there was only a sign with the name of the pass. And bikers coming up the hill were saying bonjour, not buenos dias.
Vanlife at the border: Talked with a German girl named Mora who was finishing up her phd thesis in biochemistry, sitting by her car with a solar panel and laptop.
I think I’ve ridden about 1100 miles across Spain on the last 2 trips. Making progress across Spain can be exhausting, but the country is awesome, especially the rustic backcountry and numerous friendly towns.
If you’re in the area of Barcelona, I recommend Olot, a nearby mountain town with awesome parks, small cinder cone volcanos, good food and big trees.
Tonight I’m in a small French city near the coast called Perpignan.  Fell asleep waiting for the Mariners game to start.  Next I’ll be heading up the coast to check out windsurfing spots.
