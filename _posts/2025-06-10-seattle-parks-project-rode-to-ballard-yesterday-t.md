Seattle Parks Project: Rode to Ballard yesterday to visit a few places.
Favorite stop:  playing Pickleball at Gilman Park with the local crowd.
Highlights:
Right in downtown, Bergen Place, which is in honor of Seattle’s sister city in Bergen Norway.
Kirke Park - A site of an old church and  history of the Seventh Elect Church in Israel (circa 2015), which preached celibacy, vegetarianism, long hair and beards, unless you were Daniel Salwt, the colorful leader in charge. Some great Ballard history written up here:
https://www.thefreelibrary.com/The+seventh+elect+church+in+Israel%3A+Seattle%27s+%22long-haired+preachers%22.-a0474715873
Ballard Corner’s park, in honor of corner stores that existed on many blocks and served as mini community centers.  Strange cement couch
Thyme Patch Park - a very peaceful pea patch with a shaded area tucked between houses.
Salmon Bay Park - cool forested oasis.
Loyal Heights Playfield and community center - the playfield is confusing because the soccer field is on a diagonal with baseball diamonds on both sides.
Ballard Pool - badly in need of supersizing and remodeling.
Funky Ballard neighborhoods and houses that I really haven’t slowed down to see much before  because I’m usually just passing through.  I can see why people like it there, especially around Salmon Bay  and Loyal Heights park.
