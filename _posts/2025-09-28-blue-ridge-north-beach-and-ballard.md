Blue Ridge, North Beach and Ballard
Seattle Parks ride, June 2025
My friend Ben and I started out playing pickleball at Greenlake then explored the old relic farm roads (now alleys) on the north side of the lake.  See Crescent Place  for a good waypoint and unique spot. Cool riding in there.
Headed northwest to a couple spots in Blue Ridge. What a nice neighborhood. Steep with views of the sound.  Unfortunately its beach access is private though it provided an excuse for not biking all the way down.
Biked down North Beach park and Natural area.  This is a ravine jungle that drains to the sound with a local community who takes are of it.  The ravines in Seattle are underrated, possibly because access is difficult because they are too steep, overgrown or lack trails.
sunset hill park - nice overlook.
Cute Webster park
Old school ballard community center
Wrapped up at 14th ave nw boat ramp and then played some more pickleball at Gilman, the local open play spot.
