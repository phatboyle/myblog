Seattle Parks: Carkeek chum salmon, Golden Gardens ponds, Greenlake pickleball
Loop west from my house over Maple Leaf, North Seattle CC, to see the spawning chum salmon in Piper’s creek at Carkeek park.  There are a lot of salmon in the creek. It was a nice scene with many elementary schools having picnics and the viewing spots are great.  The beavers have made some big ponds in the last few years.
South to big swoopy ride into Golden Gardens ponds and beach, then Eddie Vines boat ramp where I spend a lot of time in August and September fishing for Coho.
NW 60th Viewpoint is right where the bay opens up.
Stopped at a couple small spots near Wallingford, Corliss place (by the Good Shepard center) and Keystone place.
BTW - My Friend Derek’s Pizza in Tangletown serves square Detroit pizza  (originally cooked in old car parts pans) and  is quite good.  I miss My Brothers Pizza which used to be between the Guild 45th theaters.
Greenlake Pickleball for a few games, then home.
