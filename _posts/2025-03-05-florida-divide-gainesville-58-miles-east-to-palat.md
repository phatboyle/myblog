Florida divide, Gainesville 58 miles east to Palatka
Rode through UF, which may be built on the only hill in Florida,  to Starbucks and waited out a massive downpour.  It got sunny by 1 and was rolling on a nice bike trail toward Hawthorne with a big detour through a wildlife refuge.
I’m struck by how much standing water is in Florida, mostly as swampy forests, or slow moving creeks, sometimes on both sides of the road or trail.
After Hawthorne it was mostly on country roads.  I stayed alert for loose dogs. Mostly they were chill and the few that weren’t stayed behind a fence.
I learned that if I use the circle k app, an icy is only 85 cents. 👍
