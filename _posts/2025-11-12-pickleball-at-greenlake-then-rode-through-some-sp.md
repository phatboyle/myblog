Pickleball at Greenlake, then rode through some spots on East Queen Anne today.
Highlights:
Hooting owl in Northeast Queen Anne Green Belt at dusk.
Pea patch in Wolf Creek Ravine natural area.
Observatory Courts: odd set of tennis courts at the very top of Queen Anne by the water tower.
Northeast Queen Anne Green Belt - there are cool trails through here.  This is that big green hillside uphill from Aurora while driving along Queen Anne.
Bhy Kracke Park -  curvy trails and nooks on steep hill
Others visited:
East Queen Anne playground
MacLean Park
Trolley Hill Park
Ward Springs Park
Counterbalance Park - maybe could use some grass or fitness machines?
Thomas Wales Park
Westlake Greenbelt
Mayfair park - under construction.
