Florida forgotten coast ride, last few days: Big Bend NWR, Hurricane Helene, river otter, into Gainsville
Last couple days were through the St Marks and Big Bend NWR, campsite at the boat launch, then final 40 miles into Gainsville through farmland and small towns.
I hear a lot of rustling along the side of the road in the refuges.  I think it’s mostly small stuff and birds.  Occasionally a small splash (turtle?) and at least one big splash (alligator?)
Had a few encounters with loose dogs.  These are be sketchy especially when there is car traffic to worry about too.  I’ve found that jumping off my bike and facing them works the best as they see you as a human, instead of a rolling antelope.
Hurricane Helene did a direct hit to this region and things are still getting repaired.    It explains some of the odd things you see:  The Keaton store is now in 4 trailers that can be moved in short notice.  A fishing ship beached a half mile from the coast. Whole stands of trees in the refuge flattened. The boat launch bathroom is boarded up and the floor is covered in mud (there are temporary bathrooms next door).
The Keaton store attendant said 22’ of water came in as a big surge like a tidal wave, wiped out the store and deposited the propane tank 1/4 mile away.
Talked with a fire crew and the big bend wildlife biologist about all the controlled burns they do to improve the habitat for plants and animals.  The biologist was really impressed in how it helped the wildlife by opening up the forest and encouraging new growth.
Looked up today before crossing a bridge and saw a river otter sitting in the road.  Then it jumped off into the swamp and swam off with its group.
I’m spending the week after this ride on a Florida road trip with my wife Tracy, birdwatching at the various refuges (some that I’ve been through) and also further south in the Everglades, Big Cypress and the Keys.
