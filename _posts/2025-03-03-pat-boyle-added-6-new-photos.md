Florida Divide day 1
Riding down the middle of Florida to the Everglades over the next 10 days.  There aren’t many good places for winter rides in the US but this is one of them.
Today landed in JAX, built bike, and headed west  by bike and Uber to intersect the route.  Nice cool dry weather.  Everybody is happy.  Lots of farmland today and unlike most trips I’ve done, Florida is flat (highest point is 345 feet!)
Got a new bike (Surly Grappler) I’m testing out.
Tomorrow is about 50 miles of zig zagging towards Gainesville through farms and around a wildlife refuge.
More info:
https://bikepacking.com/routes/edt8/
