Spain-France ride: first 6 days
I’m in Spain riding north from the mountains west of Barcelona towards France.  This is a continuation of the ride I did from Tarifa (southern Spain) 18 months ago.  The route, called the European Divide Trail, goes to northern Finland. I have a ways to go.
Day 1: took train from Barcelona to Mora D’Ebre to intersect the route. Started riding, camped in some trees on the side of the road after dark.
Day 2: climb 3000’ and stayed in Montblanc
Day 3: more climbing then descent through cool canyon with castles perched on the side. Stayed in Igualada
Day 4: farms, vineyards, pea patches, cattle pastures. Camped in city park for camper vans
Day 5: stayed in Vic, a university town, at the youth hostel.
Day 6:  long ride along a huge mountain reservoir. Staying in an outdoorsy mountain town called Olot.
Was a difficult first couple days with washed out roads and steep elevation gain but I’m getting things dialed in.  This route sticks to dirt roads and national trails which are often bumpy but there are few cars to deal with. You see so much: backyard farms, vineyards, orchards, olive groves, creek beds, crumbling castles, irrigation ditches, pastures, factories.
Saw Catalonia Salt Mountain in distance.  2km salt vein that sticks up above the surface 1000 feet.  Been mined for millennia.
3 preying mantis yesterday.  Very odd creatures.
Pig farms. Seems they need to be let outside with some  space to run around.
Great weather. Just been wearing a t shirt and sometimes a Pendleton 24/7.
