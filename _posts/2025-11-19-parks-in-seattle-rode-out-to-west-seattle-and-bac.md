Parks in Seattle: Rode out to West Seattle and back from my house in NE Seattle.  (Nov 7)
First stop - Amazon free banana stand in South Lake Union
Second stop:  Epic KEXP coffee shop in Seattle Center. A model for Seattle, pacious, semi countercultural, hosts events.  Wish it  stayed open later.
High Point park pickleball: Played 1.5 hours with the friendly (but skilled) crowd at High Point. My friend Brian Bennett turned me on to the sport and this spot. My home court is at Greenlake by Evans Pool.
Made my way in a loop through West Seattle:
Myrtle Reservoir
Orchard Street Ravine - greenspace on steep slope with native plants, small forest.
Solstice Park - Cool spot. Overlook with great view of the sound.
Lincoln Park - Big trees, forest, beach, nice trails, it has it all including a high school cross country course.
Fauntleroy Creek Ravine, Kilbourne Park and Fauntleroy Park - these three spots all reveal the steep ravine of Fauntleroy creek which runs down the steep slope.  It got dark while I was bushwacking through Fauntleroy.  Coho spawning in the creek.
Endolyne park - a hillside
Roxhill park.
Peanuts and a diet coke at Five guys.
