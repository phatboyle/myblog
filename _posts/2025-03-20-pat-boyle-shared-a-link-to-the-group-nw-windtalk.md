Hello All -
Weeks of Aug 11 and 18, 2025.
This is my annual post about Andy Brandt's ABK windsurfing camps in Hood River.  If you're looking for an opportunity for great instruction at any skill level, this is the way to do it.  I've posted about this before, so pasting some info below.  Full disclosure - My family has been doing this camp for 8 years, and my kids (college age) sometimes help out at the camps.   I feel like my family lucked into finding Andy just in time when my kids were in middle school and becoming skeptical about me teaching them.  8 years later, we look forward every summer to seeing many of the same faces, and some new ones every year for a week in Hood River.
https://www.abkboardsports.com/camps
Andy’s camps are unique and a great place for sailors of all abilities to get better.  Whether you are learning to waterstart or doing high wind freestyle moves, he and his crew can help.
The  class is 5 days at the Marina in Hood River, from Monday to Friday, 9:30-5 with a 2 hour lunch.  About ⅓ of the time is spent in lectures ranging from normal progression skills such as waterstarting or jibing, to foiling and winging.  The other ⅔ is on the water.  It costs $700 and you need to bring your own gear.  Usually about 12-20 people are in the class (note that he has a reasonable cancellation policy if your plans change).
Andy offers his students choices in having fun: get better at the specific thing you want (new jibes, tacks and freestyle), a strategy to learn a new technology (winging/foiling) or a better way to have fun with the sport you already know (light wind windsurfing or freestyle).
He is a unique person and has taught for 40 years, lives in his van while teaching, and has an amazing ability to connect with all ages of people, abilities and situations.  His knowledge of windsurfing spans aerodynamics, athleticism and psychology.  Many of us think he’s one of the best teachers of any topic we’ve had.  And he’s a phenomenal sailor himself.
btw - here is a light wind freestyle video of Andy from last fall in Corpus Christi:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G-op5L1UTp0
So if you like windsurfing (or winging, or windfoiling), I’d encourage you to show up with your high wind gear, and a borrowed bigger board for lightwind practice if the wind hasn't picked up yet. It’s also a great camp to take with your kid, spouse, parent or friend because they will work with everyone at their ability level.
If the wind is light, you might spend the morning working on sailing clew first, backwinding, duck jibes, or depowering the sail for heli tacks and upwind 360s.  When the wind picks up you might find that clew first practice helps you exit your jibes a lot more reliably.  His goal is to teach a bunch of things to practice and have fun with, no matter what the conditions are.
If you’re unsure of making a week long commitment, or are worried about the wind conditions, ask around and I think you’ll hear similar stories - his clinic is worthwhile.   (I’ll admit I wish sometimes a 3 day class was offered over a weekend, instead of mon-fri).
There are lots of options for staying in Hood River. We usually camp at Tucker Park (Hood River county park).
-Pat
