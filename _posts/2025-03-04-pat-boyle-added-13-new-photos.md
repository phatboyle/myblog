Florida divide day 2
Gainsville.
Another rider called this the pac man section because of all the zig zags. Karlos, the route designer, tries hard to stay on dirt or quiet roads and off the highways.
Some locals, Shane and Mike, on gravel bikes caught up to me and we rode for a few miles.  They suggested I use a blinky red rear  light even during the day. Their reasoning is that cars tend to give even more space when passing on busier roads.  Good idea.
Related: It’s easy to forget how distracted or altered some drivers can be.  Passed a car today on a narrow dirt road. We both waved, were going really slow.  Window rolled down. Then I smelled weed. Geez
The forests grow over the dirt one lane roads and sometimes it feels like a tunnel.
You see many houses in the forests. Some bigger nice ones with big fields and animals. Some smaller ones with nice gardens.  Some shacks.  A real mix.
Mike and Shane reminded me that  U of Florida is in Gainesville. There weren’t any basketball or softball games tonight but I rode around campus and had Pho in their U district. Really nice campus with lots of people out.
Tomorrow there is some rain forecasted.  We’ll see how that goes.
