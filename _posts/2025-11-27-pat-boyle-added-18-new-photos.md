Bike ride: West Duwamish greenway trails, South Seattle Community College, Camp Long, Duwamish Longhouse.
My target was all the unvisited parks between the Duwamish river and Camp Long.  I was familiar with Delridge, but knew very little about the rest of this area, which includes Highland Park and Puget Ridge.
One highlight of the ride was Camp Long, designed and built in the 30s to be a year-around scout camp but for all youth in Seattle.  It’s well done, and built in what was steep, unusable land adjacent to the Delridge golf course.  It has cabins embedded in the forest, with fireplaces, spread around a big field.  Schurman Rock is supposedly the oldest purpose built climbing rock in the country,  designed by a scoutmaster, and lead climbing ranger at Mt Rainier (also had a hand in getting Camp Long built).  Lou Whittaker and Fred Becky trained there.  Unfortunately the lodge was burned in 2024, but it looks like they are repairing it.
“Highland park improvement club” little signs revealed where this community has been maintaining and building trails and hosting events all over.
https://hpic1919.org/
Notes:
The steep hilside making up the west slope of the Duwamish valley is a 3.5 mile long greenway with trails all througout.  The backbone is the West Duwamish Greenbelt trail, very worthwhile for an urban hike.
At the south end is the expansive Westcrest Park, adjacent to South Park, with a dogpark, pea patch, playground and running loop high up on the ridge.  Below is a  doug fir forest.
Rode north on the ridge to Riverview playfield high above the river.  For some reason the gate accessing South Seattle Community College from the greenbelt trails was locked but  someone cut a hole in the fence so I could get through.
I’d never been to South Seattle CC - the campus is quite spacious and relaxed and felt more appealing than the North Seattle and Central campuses.  There was a good fitness center and student union.
Passed through the Seattle Chinese Garden, then Puget Park, with some bushwacking up to Pigeon Point Park in the backyard of the Pathfinder K-8 school.
Headed south to Puget Ridge (park), then back north to Delridge Park and Community Center (too rainy for pickleball), past Greg Davis and Cottage Grove parks, and west up to Camp Long.
Riding home, at the Duwamish Longhouse on West Marginal Way, which also has a small museum and gift shop.  Across the street are several parks providing good shoreline access to the Duwamish and its side channels. (Herring’s House park - Tualtwx).
West Marginal way is noisy and hectic, but it’s a working area with a train, shipping terminals, diving companies, a few funky houses, “college hunks” haul away company, and recycling factories.  Worthwhile to poke around.
