Dec 3: Beacon Hill, Flora Bakehouse, New Holly, Dearborn, Seward and Lake Washington blvd parks
Met my neighbor, a former arborist, now anesthesiologist for a tour of spots scattered south and east of Beacon Hill.
First stop was the Flora Bakehouse. A really good bakery, and makes everything on site.  I’m still puzzled why good bagettes and croissants are $5 in Seattle and $1.25 in France.
Most underrated place on this ride - Martha Washington Park a biggish park south of Seward park with a long rustic beach and remnants of an orchard.
Maple School Ravine - dischevelled hillside of blackberries, etc
John C Little Sr Park -  Named after a park commissioner who started an in-city 4H program. Near New Holly, a big, interesting in-city development.
Dearborn Park - Tucked into a hillside it has pickleball courts
Seward Park - Obviously beloved by everybody.  We rode the 3 mile loop and climbed up to the top.  Nice trails.
Two hand launch boat launches: Ferdinand Street and Adams Street boat launches are wide spots on Lake Washington Boulevard.
And Lakewood Playground.
