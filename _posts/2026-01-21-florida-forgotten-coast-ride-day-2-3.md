Florida forgotten coast ride. Day 2-3
Rode east through St Marks wildlife refuge today.  Well, actually I needed to detour because they were doing “Arial hog remediation” which means shooting feral hogs from helicopters  to keep them from destroying the refuge.
Tonight staying at “Scout Lodge”, deep in the woods along a river and the bay.  The owners do a good job keeping the place running despite hurricanes and periodic flooding.
St Marks town is celebrating its 500th anniversary in 2028.  Panfillo de Narvaez from Spain came in search of Aztec gold with roughly 300 people and 10 ships.  Only 4 survived and took 6 years to turn up near Mazatlan.  200 years later a Spanish fort was built.
Tomorrow I head south east through big bend wildlife refuge.
