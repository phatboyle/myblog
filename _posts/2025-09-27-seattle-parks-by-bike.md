Seattle Parks by bike:
Last week I took a tour on the west side of Queen Anne.  Because it is so steep, it has some really cool parks and places to visit.   btw - yes, I’m taking a while to whittle away on this project (It’s been over a year so far) but I’m pretty happy with taking it slow and the discoveries of each excursion.
David Rogers Park stands out. It’s a big forested area above the Queen Anne bowl with embedded playgrounds and trails. Worthwhile.
Sound View Terrace, is tiny and sandwiched between two streets overlooking Magnolia.
The Southwest Queen Anne greenbelt is that steep hillside that stretches on the west side for a mile along Elliot. You may not even notice unless you look up into the jungle.  There are some campsites in there but also some neat trails.
Kinnear Park is on the south end of the greenbelt and even has a small pickleball court, so I joined in with the locals.
This area has a lot of neat features, stairs, split level streets contouring the side of the hill, neat old homes and great views.  It’s also steep and a great workout.
Others visited:
Bayview/Kerry, which is a Seattle classic with an overlook view of the Seattle Center.
Queen Anne Boulevard and Marshall, Parsons Gardens - a great walk, especially in the evening.
West Queen Anne Playfield, community center and pool
Coe Play Park (a playground in an elementary school)
