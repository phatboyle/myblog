Go Mariners!  Games start around 2 am my time but I can wake up at 4:30 to catch the last 9 innings 🤔
Last night I rolled through a small town, expecting it to be quiet like the last few towns, and they were  having a sat night autumn festival.
Camped by a mountain church and got up uncertain when anything would be open for a  coffee. But then I biked by a farm having a middle and high school “horse ball” competition (basketball with horses) with food and coffee stand.
Solo travel combined with these backroad rides is not for everybody but one thing I like is that you have no idea what will happen next.
Last few days: headed onto Leucete on the coast to check out the windsurfing. It was windy and managed to find a rental shop at the beach  so went out for an hour.  That was fun and many people from all over Europe were there.
Camped in a field with soft grass on the side of the route.
In the morning, to my surprise,  I ran into another rider, Alexey from Dublin, on the same route at the coffee shop in the morning. He was heading south and we compared notes.
Stayed in Beziers at a hotel built in an old 140 year old prison that was closed in 2009. Creepy but really nicely remodeled.  Beziers is in wine country and is a cool town with 10 locks on the 500 mile long canal that goes all the way to western France. Sun am there was a 6000 person race in starting and ending in central square.
Then two days riding NW to Ales, small city on the way to Valence.
